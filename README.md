HDFC Bank Equity Research
Project Overview

An independent equity research project focused on HDFC Bank, covering financial performance analysis, risk assessment, forecasting, valuation, and investment thesis development.

The project was developed as a practical equity research workflow using Excel for financial modelling and analysis, supported by a research report and investor presentation.

Project Objectives
Analyse HDFC Bank's historical financial performance.
Evaluate key growth and profitability indicators.
Assess asset quality, risk and capital strength.
Analyse loan and deposit growth trends.
Develop forward-looking financial assumptions.
Perform scenario-based valuation.
Formulate an investment thesis based on the analysis.
Research Workflow

Company → Financials → Risk → Peers → Forecast → Valuation → Investment Thesis
